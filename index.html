You can watch the lessons online at your own pace, rewatch them whenever you need, and use the Academy as a long-term reference before your next mission.

You also get 6 months free access to the Ultimate Fishing channel, worth $55, which includes hundreds of hours of ad-free on-demand fishing content, early access to new ITM Fishing Show episodes, monthly educational live streams with Matt and special guests, plus member-only gear discounts like 10% off Wise Angler.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this right for me?

Yes — and it's probably the best investment you'll make as a beginner. Because you can build the right habits from the start instead of spending years guessing, wasting money on the wrong gear, or relying on random advice.

Matt starts with the foundations, including gear selection, knots, rigs, bait and lure presentation, reading the water, understanding tides, using your fish finder, and knowing where to start when you get on the water. Everything is explained in a practical, step-by-step way, so you won't be thrown in the deep end.

The goal is to help you understand what you're doing and why, not just copy a few tricks. By the end, you'll have a clear system to follow, more confidence when choosing where and how to fish, and a much better chance of turning blank sessions into successful days on the water.

I've been fishing for years. Will I still get value from this?

Almost certainly.

In fact, many of the anglers who get the most value from this course are the ones who already consider themselves experienced. Most people pick up fishing through trial and error, advice from mates, and years on the water, but that doesn't always mean they've been taught the full system behind why things work.

Matt breaks down the details that many experienced anglers still have gaps in, including how to read conditions properly, find fish more consistently, choose the right rigs and presentations, tie stronger knots, and get more from your fish finder.

A lot of anglers can catch fish when everything lines up. This course is designed to help you understand what to do when it doesn't, so you can make better decisions and fish with more confidence.

What kind of fishing does the course cover?

The course is built around proven saltwater fishing techniques that can be applied anywhere in the world. It is not limited to one location, one species, or one style of fishing. Instead, Matt teaches the core skills that help you become a more capable, adaptable angler in any saltwater environment.

You'll learn how to find fish, read conditions, choose the right setups, understand rigs, present baits and lures properly, use your boat & sounder with more confidence, and make smarter decisions every time you head out.

Whether you fish from a boat or land, the principles remain the same. The conditions may change, but the techniques, decision-making, and confidence you build in the course are designed to travel with you.

Why not just watch YouTube fishing videos for free?
YouTube is random. You get disconnected tips from dozens of different people with different techniques, locations and skill levels — and no way to know which advice to trust or how it fits together. This course is a structured progression built by one person who has spent 20 years mastering his craft. Matt builds on each lesson so by the end you have a complete, coherent system — not a pile of unrelated clips.

How is this different from Matt's TV show?
The TV show is entertainment first — it captures the action and the catch. This course is education first. Matt sits down and teaches you exactly what he's doing and why — the thinking behind every decision, the rigging details, the reading of conditions. It's the stuff that never makes it to air because it doesn't make for exciting television, but it's exactly what makes the difference between a good day and a blank one.
I've seen some of Matt's educational clips before. What's the difference?

So while you may have seen Matt share advice before, this is a much more detailed & structured way to learn long term.
